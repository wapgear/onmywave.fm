onmywave.fm 0.5.1 makes the listening sidebars consistent.

- DJ, Queue, and Lyrics share the same sidebar width, rounded layout, and compact header styling. Sidebar headers match the song-list header's height and colors across themes.
- Lyrics open in the sidebar. Use Expand to fill the content area and the return control to bring lyrics back to the sidebar.
- All three sidebars share the opening and closing animation. Switching follows the bottom-button order, Lyrics → Queue → DJ, with reversible sliding and soft edge fades. Reduced-motion preferences are respected.
- The accent highlight identifies only the open sidebar. DJ active/inactive status uses different icon shapes, with a tooltip and accessible description. All three buttons are neutral when their sidebars are closed.

Requires an Apple silicon Mac running macOS 12 or later and your own Jellyfin server. The app is Developer ID signed and notarized by Apple.

Download `onmywave-0.5.1-arm64.dmg`, open it, and drag onmywave.fm into Applications. Existing updater-enabled installations can update from Settings → General → About and updates.

The source archive contains the corresponding build inputs and license notices. `SHA256SUMS.txt` contains checksums for downloads and updater files. Verification used the real renderer with a fictional library, including sidebar geometry, lyrics expansion and following, keyboard focus, directional switching, rapid reversal, reduced motion, DJ icon states, and header alignment across light, dark, and coffee themes. Motion was also checked in Electron 43.8.1. Live Jellyfin streaming, VoiceOver, and an update between installed releases were not exercised for this release.
