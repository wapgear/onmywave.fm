# onmywave.fm 0.7.9

- Automatic news preparation is selected by default for new rooms and starts after you save the setup.
- Failed or interrupted news checks stay in history. The next check starts on schedule with a fresh request after an interruption.
- The News bucket shows which daily limit was reached and opens the setting to increase it when possible. Checks become eligible again after local midnight.
- App updates, news recovery, and library warnings share a consistent location in the app header.

Signed and notarized for Apple silicon Macs running macOS 13 or later. The DMG supports manual installation, and existing installations can update in the app.
