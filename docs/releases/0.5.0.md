onmywave.fm 0.5.0 gives your DJ a dedicated sidebar.

- Open Your DJ from the radio icon beside lyrics and queue at the bottom right. Review the current story, sources, profile, schedule and listening history without a popover attached to the player.
- Pause, resume or stop a DJ break inside the sidebar. When it is closed, active break controls return to a compact floating notice above the player.
- The DJ sidebar slides in and out, respects reduced-motion preferences, and shares the existing close, Escape and focus-return behavior with the other listening sidebars.
- Player notices use consistent floating styling without shrinking the library. Focus remains stable through DJ actions and failed settings saves.
- The loaded-song count floats at the library's bottom right while scrolling, fading away after scrolling stops.

Requires an Apple silicon Mac running macOS 12 or later and your own Jellyfin server. The app is Developer ID signed and notarized by Apple.

Download `onmywave-0.5.0-arm64.dmg`, open it, and drag onmywave.fm into Applications. Existing updater-enabled installations can update from Settings → General → About and updates.

The source archive contains the corresponding build inputs and license notices. `SHA256SUMS.txt` contains checksums for downloads and updater files. UI checks used the built renderer with a synthetic library and local audio, including sidebar controls, keyboard focus, error recovery, reduced motion, search and scrolling. Live Jellyfin streaming, VoiceOver, and an update between installed releases were not exercised for this release.
