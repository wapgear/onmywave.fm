# onmywave.fm 0.8.0

- Open the new Appearance tab to compare Light, Coffee, and Dark with 16:9 app previews.
- Use cover art for the Now Playing backdrop, the accent color, or both. The backdrop fades between songs after the next cover is ready.
- Open artists and albums directly from song details and library links. Choose whether album and artist playback stays in the collection or mixes with your library.
- Use the pointer cursor on playback sliders and the two-handle DJ song-range controls.

Signed and notarized for Apple silicon Macs running macOS 13 or later. Install from the DMG or update from within the app.
