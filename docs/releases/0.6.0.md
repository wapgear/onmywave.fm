onmywave.fm 0.6.0 adds configurable DJ song introductions and a quieter settings experience.

- Enable song introductions after a configurable number of completed songs, defaulting to six, or after every fact-only news bulletin. Announcements are off by default and have their own interval independent of news.
- Introductions use the actual upcoming queue track and saved DJ voice. Queue changes cancel stale speech; skipping a song does not count as a completed song.
- New news recordings end on their final fact, with one playback-owned transition into the next track. Older recordings keep their existing ending without an extra introduction.
- Find news and song timing together at the top of Settings → General. Explanations live behind info buttons, forms share consistent spacing, Sources and DJs use compact subsection headings, and the transition sample stays collapsed until needed.
- News can read linked article context when a feed has too little evidence, while retaining saved selection and writing progress.
- Updated Electron and other runtime dependencies. **Requires an Apple silicon Mac running macOS 13 or later**, plus your own Jellyfin server.

The macOS downloads are Developer ID signed and notarized by Apple. Download `onmywave-0.6.0-arm64.dmg`, open it, and drag onmywave.fm into Applications. Supported updater-enabled installations can update from Settings → General → About and updates.

The corresponding source archive, updater metadata, blockmaps and `SHA256SUMS.txt` are included. Verification used synthetic metadata and audio, with 32 native announcement playback scenarios, settings and keyboard checks, main-process handoff integration, 22 editorial checks, and quality/security checks. No live model or speech requests were made. Real voice pronunciation and continuity, physical audio devices, native VoiceOver, and an update between installed release versions were not exercised.
