onmywave.fm 0.7.3 brings the cumulative settings and design-consistency improvements onto 0.7.2.

- Separate General preferences from Playback, with compact News & voice, Song announcements and DJ transitions sections. Each form retains its own draft and save action.
- Choose varied, warm, upbeat, short or custom song-introduction wording. Text examples use no speech credits; enabled announcements retain their existing provider cost and cancellation behavior.
- Share navigation, preference layouts and collection actions while preserving the existing typography, colors and compact controls.
- Keep sorting controls together, improve queue reorder targets and sidebar keyboard focus, explain source limits and URL formats, and preserve expanded Lyrics pane semantics.
- Keep transition previews open during timing edits while stopping the old audio. Preserve story context, responsive reading space and visible News categories.

Verification uses synthetic content and fixture providers. See [the consistency audit](../verification/design-consistency/README.md) for screenshots, executable rules, manual checks and limitations.
