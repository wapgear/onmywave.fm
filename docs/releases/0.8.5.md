# onmywave.fm 0.8.5

- All Songs loads up to 200 tracks at a time. With one selected library, Jellyfin can filter removed tracks before returning a page, so opening the app can show songs without downloading the whole catalog.
- The All Songs loading screen is centered and shows two randomly chosen facts from a collection of 50 music facts.

Install from the DMG or update from within the app on an Apple silicon Mac running macOS 13 or later.
