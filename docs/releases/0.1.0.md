onmywave.fm 0.1.0 adds song lyrics from your Jellyfin library.

Open the microphone icon beside Queue to show lyrics across the song-list area. Timed lyrics highlight and scroll with the song, with smooth motion and fades at the top and bottom. Click a timed line, or use Enter or Space, to jump to that part of the song. Manual scrolling pauses following; Follow current line resumes it. Reduced-motion settings use instant scrolling without line animations. Plain lyrics retain their line breaks.

Lyrics require Jellyfin 10.9 or later and lyric files available on the server. To download missing lyrics, install a compatible Jellyfin LrcLib Lyrics plugin, enable it for your music library, and run Download missing lyrics in Scheduled Tasks. The app displays saved server lyrics and does not download them itself. Availability depends on your library metadata and the lyrics provider. Word-level highlighting and separate lyric metadata offsets are not supported.

This release also includes the centered play icon from the unreleased 0.0.3 source version.

Requires an Apple silicon Mac running macOS 12 or later and your own Jellyfin server. The app is Developer ID signed and notarized by Apple.

Download `onmywave-0.1.0-arm64.dmg`, open it, and drag onmywave.fm into Applications. Existing updater-enabled installations can update from Settings → General → About and updates.

The source archive contains the corresponding build inputs and license notices. `SHA256SUMS.txt` contains checksums for downloads and updater files. Optional news and generated voice features can incur provider charges. Automated checks used fictional songs and local HTTP/media fixtures. Live Jellyfin streaming, VoiceOver and an update between installed releases were not exercised for this release.
