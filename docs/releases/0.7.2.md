onmywave.fm 0.7.2 refines news controls and settings layouts.

- Give news playback controls comfortable padding and a clean hover effect.
- Use one consistent hover highlight across each news story.
- Balance select text and chevron insets, and animate chevrons when menus open. Reduced-motion preferences are respected.
- Center General and News settings in a readable column that uses its full available width, up to 760px.

Requires an Apple silicon Mac running macOS 13 or later, plus your own Jellyfin server. Optional news preparation uses your configured providers and can incur charges.

Download `onmywave-0.7.2-arm64.dmg`, open it, and drag onmywave.fm into Applications. Updater-enabled installations can update from Settings → General → About and updates.

The macOS downloads are Developer ID signed and notarized by Apple. The corresponding source archive, updater metadata, blockmaps and `SHA256SUMS.txt` are included.

Controls and layouts were verified with synthetic content at widths of 800, 1280 and 1600px, across Dark, Light and Coffee themes. Playback controls, select animations, keyboard focus and reduced motion were exercised. Native VoiceOver and an update between installed release versions were not exercised.
