onmywave.fm 0.5.4 makes the News bucket easier to browse, read, and configure.

- Browse Upcoming, Check news, Past news, and Favorites from a simple sidebar. Source and preparation configuration lives in News settings.
- Story rows show publication dates and saved excerpts. Wider windows include a reading pane; compact windows provide a focused reader with Back to stories. Opening a story never starts playback.
- Save favorite articles independently of their recordings. Past news separates completed playback for the current room from all saved recordings, scripts, and editorial reviews.
- Configure automatic preparation, sources, schedule, and writing in one place. Provider and source management returns to the same setup with drafts retained.
- A flush workspace, aligned controls, consistent spacing, and quieter typography fit the rest of the app across Dark, Light, and Coffee themes.

Requires an Apple silicon Mac running macOS 12 or later and your own Jellyfin server. The macOS app is Developer ID signed and notarized by Apple.

Download `onmywave-0.5.4-arm64.dmg`, open it, and drag onmywave.fm into Applications. Existing updater-enabled installations can update from Settings → General → About and updates.

The source archive contains the corresponding build inputs and license notices. `SHA256SUMS.txt` contains checksums for downloads and updater files. News Bucket verification used synthetic stories and simulated audio at six viewport/theme combinations, covering navigation, keyboard focus, favorites, playback preview, setup recovery, responsive layout, and sampled text contrast. No live model or speech requests were made for verification. Native VoiceOver, physical audio devices, and an update between installed releases were not exercised.
