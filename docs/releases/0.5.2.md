onmywave.fm 0.5.2 makes lyrics expansion smooth and keeps its controls in place.

- Expand, return, and close controls stay at the same screen position in both lyrics modes. The expanded card keeps the sidebar's header styling, top and right insets, and clearance above the player.
- Lyrics expand and collapse with a 220 ms live resize. Text reflows without being stretched, and rapid toggles smoothly reverse the transition. Reduced-motion preferences are respected.
- The library and player stay stable while lyrics resize. Focus, loaded lyrics, and manual scrolling state are preserved.
- Synced lyrics keep following the current line during resizing, including narrow windows. A stale scroll-end event no longer cancels a newer follow request.

Requires an Apple silicon Mac running macOS 12 or later and your own Jellyfin server. The app is Developer ID signed and notarized by Apple.

Download `onmywave-0.5.2-arm64.dmg`, open it, and drag onmywave.fm into Applications. Existing updater-enabled installations can update from Settings → General → About and updates.

The source archive contains the corresponding build inputs and license notices. `SHA256SUMS.txt` contains checksums for downloads and updater files. Verification used the real renderer with a fictional library at four viewport sizes, checking exact control positions during resizing, stable layout, lyric following, rapid reversal, manual scrolling, focus, reduced motion, and interruption by switching or closing. Desktop and narrow-window behavior were also checked in Electron 43.8.1. Live Jellyfin streaming, VoiceOver, and an update between installed releases were not exercised for this release.
