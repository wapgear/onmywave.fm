# Listening Room

The Listening Room configures a music host and prepares news audio before it is played.

## Language

**Source**:
A named RSS or Atom news feed chosen by the listener.

**DJ**:
A saved host identity with personality, language, instructions and voice preferences.

**Station mood**:
The listener's description of the atmosphere and delivery they want for the station.
_Avoid_: Measured song mood

**Listening Room setup**:
The listener's chosen sources, DJ and station mood.

**News draft**:
A short editable script prepared from chosen news sources, with its source references and the setup used to prepare it.

**Audio take**:
A complete saved recording of a reviewed news script, with the voice and setup used when it was prepared.
_Avoid_: Live news stream

**Selected take**:
The saved audio take explicitly chosen by the listener for a news break. Editing setup or preparing another take does not change this choice.

**News break**:
Playback of a prepared audio take over a quiet music bed near the end of a song.

**Workflow draft**:
The listener's saved choices for what matters, who hosts, and when briefings should be prepared. A draft does not activate automatic preparation.

**Workflow run**:
One scheduled check that either finds no qualifying update, prepares a briefing, or records why it could not complete.

**Important update**:
A fresh development significant enough for the listener's chosen news interests and importance setting. It is an editorial decision with a reason, not a promise that every major event will be found.

**Morning briefing**:
A short start-of-day segment based on fresh weather for the listener's chosen place and day. Calendar and reminders may contribute later with the listener's permission.

**Ready briefing**:
A complete saved recording available to listen to. Being ready does not mean it has played or replaced the selected take.
